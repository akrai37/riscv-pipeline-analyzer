## Headline numbers

- Forwarding removes 92.4% of data-dependency stall cycles (data-hazard stalls without it vs load-use stalls with it) and 82.1% of all stall cycles, across 7 workloads.
- Geometric-mean CPI: 1.660 without forwarding, 1.126 with (2-bit predictor in both); 1.763 with neither forwarding nor a direction predictor.
